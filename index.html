Chatham First Light

Chatham Light has swept this shoreline for eighty years. Help us keep it that way.

This July, the Coast Guard replaced Chatham Light's beacon with a new LED unit. The old light cast a wide, unmistakable sweep across the trees, the streets, and the water — a signal boaters and residents have oriented by for generations. The new light doesn't do that. Near shoals that shift as often as Chatham's, that's not a matter of nostalgia. It's a matter of what mariners can actually see.

What we're asking for

We're not asking the Coast Guard to leave the light as it was during a pandemic-era generator swap, or to resist modern equipment on principle. We're asking for the historic apparatus to be fitted with a modern LED source and drive mechanism that restores the light's full sweep — the same aura mariners and residents have relied on for decades, delivered with current technology.

This isn't an Old Village concern alone. It affects the whole town — its shoreline, its waterways, its beaches, and everyone offshore who has used that light to find their way home.

Chatham First Light is a group of residents, boaters, and fishermen raising this with the town first — the Select Board, the Historical Society, the Historic Commission — and then, if needed, with our Congressional delegation and a formal letter to the Coast Guard Commandant. Every name on this list becomes part of that record.

Sign the record

Your name and town may be shared with the Select Board, Historical Society, and Coast Guard as part of this campaign's record. Street addresses are used only for that formal record and aren't published anywhere.

Chatham First Light is an independent resident effort and is not affiliated with the U.S. Coast Guard, the Town of Chatham, or the Chatham Historical Society. This page collects signatures in support of restoring Chatham Light's beam and will be shared with those groups as part of the campaign.
